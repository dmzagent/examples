How this desk works

  1. Run python3 app/simulate.py --loop 30 in another terminal: three agents talk and act; one leaks personal data, one misuses a tool.
  2. Reasoning tags what they did. The governance in governance.py turns tags into a hold (recoverable), an open breaker (a hard stop), a review for you, an escalation, or a remediation directive.
  3. Claim a review, confirm or dismiss it (a dismissal weakens the tag on the subject's soul), and release the subject. Every transition below carries a ledger anchor.
